ADB pairing and transport are based on Mika v0.1.37 by wangzan101.

https://github.com/wangzan101/Mika/tree/v0.1.37/core/src/main/kotlin/com/wangzan101/mika/core/adb

The Android identity adapter, bounded transport reads and loopback ADB discovery were adapted for this app. The discovery follows the local-port approach in Mika's LocalPortScan.kt.
